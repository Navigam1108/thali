# Suggested project title

Thali - Unified Nutrition Tracking for the Eternal Ecosystem

# Problem Description

Indian consumers often eat across homemade meals, restaurant orders and packaged snacks, but recording a complete day requires repetitive entry, recipe estimates and label calculations. Household variations in ingredients, oil and portions make generic food entries unreliable. The problem we aim to solve is the effort of building a useful, consistent nutrition diary across these sources.

We propose Thali, an AI assisted nutrition companion for Eternal's ecosystem. A meal photo suggests likely dishes; users confirm foods and portions to obtain approximate calorie and macro ranges. Ingredient-based recipes, user-confirmed Zomato order entries and reviewed snack-label scans complete the daily diary. A community recipe library lets people share and reuse Indian dishes with visible ingredients, portions and sources. Building on Zomato's nutrition discovery, Thali could later connect these preferences to meal recommendations and optional Blinkit ingredient baskets. Our MVP uses real on-device image inference, illustrative nutrition references, sample orders and browser storage. Pilot testing will measure logging effort, recognition and nutrient error, and recipe reuse.
